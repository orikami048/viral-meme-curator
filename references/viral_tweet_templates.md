# Viral U.S. Tweet & Meme Formatting Templates

Use these structural templates when converting raw Reddit/X posts into high-engagement viral tweets.

---

## Template 1: "Bro Really Thought" (Disbelief / Irony)

```text
Bro really thought [action] 💀

[Image or Meme GIF]
```

*Example*:
> Bro really thought he could fix his sleep schedule by going to bed at 5 AM 💀

---

## Template 2: "Nobody / Absolutely Nobody" (Unprovoked Action)

```text
Nobody:
Absolutely nobody:
[Target Group/Person]: [Absurd Action] 😭
```

*Example*:
> Nobody:
> Absolutely nobody:
> Crypto bros at 3 AM: "We are so back 🚀"

---

## Template 3: "POV" (Point of View)

```text
POV: You just [relatable situation] 💀
```

*Example*:
> POV: You opened Twitter for 5 minutes and lost 1000 aura

---

## Template 4: "My Honest Reaction" (Reaction Image / GIF)

```text
[Hot Take or Absurd Situation]

My honest reaction: 🗿
```

---

## Template 5: "We Are So Back vs It's So Over" (Rollercoaster Sentiment)

```text
[Bad News]: It's so over...
[Slight Good News 5 min later]: WE ARE SO BACK 🔥
```
